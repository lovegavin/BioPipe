# Operon 1.88.0

One hard capability: **`sequence_rna_cofold`** (677 operators, 101 Readers).
It cofolds two RNA strands with ViennaRNA `RNAcofold -p -c` and reports the
dimer MFE, ensemble energy, binding free energy, MFE frequency and the
equilibrium concentrations of the five species (AB, AA, BB, A, B).

## Operator

- Input: a `[sequence, position]` RNA Form with exactly two sequences.
- Parameters: `concentration_a`, `concentration_b` (micromolar, must be
  positive), `no_lonely_pairs`, `rnacofold_path`, `timeout_seconds`,
  `dimer_dim`, `output`.
- Output `[dimer, statistic]`: `mfe`, `ensemble`, `frequency`, `binding`,
  `conc_ab`, `conc_aa`, `conc_bb`, `conc_a`, `conc_b`; info `structure`,
  `sequences`, `concentrations`, `n_duplex`.

## Boundaries

- Concentrations follow RNAcofold's five-species equilibrium model; the values
  are model predictions, not experimental measurements.
- Only two strands are supported; longer complexes belong in a dedicated
  multi-strand wrapper.
