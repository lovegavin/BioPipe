# Operon 1.122.7

## Fixed

- The Boltz environment is a `venv --system-site-packages` on top of the shared
  interpreter with only `boltz==2.2.1` installed (`--no-deps`). It inherits the
  exact validated scientific stack, keeps Boltz's strict metadata pins out of the
  shared `pip check`, and needs no second solve that would hit the unavailable
  `fairscale` mirror or `modelcif` conflicts. The installer branches on
  `venv_shared`, `clone_shared` or a fresh micromamba environment.
- `tests/test_remote_provision.py` stubs the isolated-environment step.
