# Operon 1.69.0

Tenth real-computation batch: **PROPKA protonation states** through pdb2pqr,
with per-residue pKa and charge reconciliation. The library grows to
**658 operators and 101 Readers**.

## Added

- `structure_protonation_pka` (`StructureProtonationPkaStep`, category
  Structure, arity 1, output `[residue, statistic]`):
  - writes the structure as PDB and runs `pdb2pqr30 --ff=AMBER
    --titration-state-method=propka --with-ph=<pH>`;
  - parses the PROPKA summary from the run log and the per-residue atom charges
    from the PQR, then reports `pka`, `charge_hh` (Henderson-Hasselbalch) and
    `charge_pqr` (sum of PQR atom charges) for every titratable residue.
  - Parameters: `atom_dim`, `field_dim`, `ph`, `pdb2pqr_path`,
    `timeout_seconds`, `residue_dim`, `output`.

## Evidence

- Toolchain: pdb2pqr **3.8.0** with PROPKA **3.5.1** (`--titration-state-method
  propka --with-ph=7.0`), installed from PyPI.
- VHH chain A: 34 titratable residues, net charge **-2.81** (HH) versus
  **-3.00** (PQR), 0.55 s.
- Hen lysozyme chains L/M: 74 residues, net charge **15.49** versus **15.00**;
  **Glu35 pKa 6.87 / 7.03** and Asp52 **4.91 / 4.76** — the textbook
  charge-relay pattern of hen lysozyme, which is the biological sanity check.
- `pytest tests/test_extension298.py`: 5 passed (parser, HH hand cases, PQR
  parsing, validation, real pdb2pqr run).

## Boundaries

- PROPKA is an empirical predictor, not a constant-pH simulation; per-residue
  HH and PQR charges can differ by up to ~0.5 because pdb2pqr assigns discrete
  protonation states.
- One pH per run; no pH titration curve, no explicit-solvent pKa, no tautomer
  enumeration for histidine.
