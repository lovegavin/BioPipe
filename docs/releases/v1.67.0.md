# Operon 1.67.0

Eighth real-computation batch: a new **MM/GBSA** operator that estimates a
binding-energy delta from three OpenMM single-point evaluations and decomposes
it into bonded, nonbonded and generalized-Born terms. The library grows to
**656 operators and 101 Readers**.

## Added

- `interface_mmgbsa_delta` (`InterfaceMmgbsaDeltaStep`, category Structure,
  arity 1, output `[term, statistic]`):
  - repairs the complex with PDBFixer and builds `amber14-all.xml` +
    `implicit/gbn2.xml` systems for the complex and for each side;
  - assigns one OpenMM force group per force so `getState(groups={i})` yields
    per-term energies, classified as `bonded`, `nonbonded`, `gb`, `other`;
  - columns `complex_kj`, `binder_kj`, `target_kj`, `delta_kj`, rows for the
    four terms plus `total`; `info` carries particle counts, chain lists and the
    total/nonbonded/GB deltas.
  - Parameters: `atom_dim`, `field_dim`, `binder_chains`, `target_chains`,
    `forcefield`, `implicit_model`, `ph`, `term_dim`, `output`.

## Evidence

- Real run on the 1MEL complex, binder `A,B` (two VHH copies) versus target
  `L,M`: 7596 / 3762 / 3834 particles, 4.8 s on CPU.
- Energies (kJ/mol): complex **8194.76**, binder **9318.68**, target
  **-1198.10**, delta **74.18**.
- Decomposition of the delta: bonded **0.00** (exact cancellation), nonbonded
  **-4700.40**, GB **+4774.58** (desolvation penalty); the two terms sum to the
  total delta, and each term's three components sum to its complex energy.
- `pytest tests/test_extension296.py`: 4 passed (real run included).

## Boundaries

- Single-point MM/GBSA without minimization, entropy or explicit solvent; it is
  a scoring heuristic, not a free-energy method. Positive delta here reflects
  the unrelaxed crystal interface, not a biological conclusion.
- CPU platform only; the ligand/variant decomposition (per-residue) is not part
  of this operator.
