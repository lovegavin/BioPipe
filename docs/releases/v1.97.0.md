# Operon 1.97.0

One hard capability: **`esm3_inverse_folding`** (686 operators, 101 Readers).
It designs a sequence for a protein backbone with the official ESM-3 open model,
conditioning on coordinates only, and reports recovery against the native
sequence.

## Operator

- Parameters: `structure_path` (PDB), `chain`, `num_steps` (default 20),
  `temperature`, `device`, `design_dim`, `output`.
- Output `[design, statistic]`: `n_residues`, `recovery`; info `sequences`,
  `native`, `n_residues`, `recovery`, `num_steps`, `temperature`, `chain`,
  `model`, `structure_path`.
- The chain is extracted into a temporary PDB, the native sequence is kept for
  recovery, `ESMProtein.sequence` is masked, and the model runs
  `GenerationConfig(track="sequence", condition_on_coordinates_only=True)`.

## Verification

  `VQLQASGGGSVQAGGSLRLS...`, **recovery 0.788 in 20.2 s**.
- Registry check: 686 operators / 101 Readers / 7 packages / 24 routes,
  `errors []`.

## Boundaries

- Single chain only; multi-chain and ligand-conditioned design are not wired.
- Weights are the open 1.4 B ESM-3 checkpoint delivered through the Hugging Face
  mirror (`HF_ENDPOINT` documented in `docs/large_resources.md`).
- Recovery is a sanity metric, not a quality score; absolute quality needs
  structure re-prediction and experimental validation.
