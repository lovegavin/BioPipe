# Operon backend 1.7.0

Backend-only release. Application version **1.7.0**; SDK **3.1.0**; protocol
**v3**. 432 operators, 92 Readers, seven provider packages and 24 route
templates.

## Added capabilities

| Capability | Provider | Input | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `moran` | `operon-core` | values `[sample, feature]` + distance `[sample, sample_b]` | `[statistic, value]` |
| `indicator_species` | `operon-microbiome` | abundance `[sample, taxon]` + metadata `[sample, feature]` | `[taxon, statistic]` |
| `pairwise_alignment` | `operon-core` | two sequences `[sequence, position]` | `[statistic, value]` |
| `PdbmlReader` | `operon-readers` | `.pdbml` file | `[atom, field]` |

`moran` computes Moran's I of one value column with row-standardised weights
(`inverse` distance or `binary` adjacency), reports the observed statistic, the
expectation `-1 / (n - 1)`, the normal-approximation standard deviation and the
two-sided p-value, matching `ape::Moran.I`.

`indicator_species` computes the Dufrene-Legendre IndVal for every taxon and
group: fidelity (share of group samples where the taxon occurs), specificity
(group mean over the sum of group means) and their product, matching
`labdsv::indval` with `numitr = 0`. No permutation p-value is produced.

`pairwise_alignment` aligns two sequences with affine gaps using Gotoh's
dynamic program. Gap cost is `gap_open + (k - 1) * gap_extend`, matching the
Biopython `PairwiseAligner` convention. The output reports the score, identity,
matches, mismatches, gap opens, gap bases and lengths; the aligned strings are
recorded in the output metadata.

`PdbmlReader` parses PDBML `atom_site` records and mirrors the fields of
`PdbReader` (`x`, `y`, `z`, `occupancy`, `bfactor`, `residue_number`, `chain`,
`resname`, `element`) with the same `[atom, field]` layout and encoder support.
`model = 0` reads every model, otherwise only the requested model.

## Oracle evidence

| Check | Reference | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moran's I | R `ape::Moran.I` | observed, expectation and sd match to `1e-9` |
| Indicator species | R `labdsv::indval(numitr = 0)` | fidelity, specificity and IndVal match to `1e-6` |
| Pairwise score | Biopython `Bio.Align.PairwiseAligner` | global and local scores match for four pairs |
| PDBML fields | Biopython `Bio.PDB.PDBMLParser` | coordinates, occupancy and B-factor match |

## Limits

- `moran` requires at least four samples and one value column; constant values
  and zero total weight are rejected. Weighting is `inverse` or `binary`; the
  row-standardised normal approximation of `ape::Moran.I` is used, not randomisation.
- `indicator_species` needs at least two groups and reports the `numitr = 0`
  IndVal output without permutation p-values. Every taxon must occur in at least
  one sample.
- `pairwise_alignment` aligns exactly two sequences with affine gaps and refuses
  matrices larger than `max_cells` (default 4,000,000). The alignment is
  deterministic; when several optimal alignments exist, the traceback order is
  fixed but may differ from Biopython's choice of witness.
- `PdbmlReader` parses `atom_site` records only; ANISOU and non-coordinate
  categories are skipped. Missing coordinate fields raise a reader error.
