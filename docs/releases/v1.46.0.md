# Operon 1.46.0

Expansion batch: ProteinMPNN robustness, embedding probes, metal sites and
transmembrane segments.

## New operators

- `proteinmpnn_noise_robustness` — re-runs the official ProteinMPNN runner on
  backbone coordinates perturbed with Gaussian noise of a given RMSD (Å) and
  reports the mean design score, global score and sequence recovery per noise
  level and replicate. The atom table is rewritten in place (all non-coordinate
  columns preserved) so the runner sees a normal PDB file; the random generator
  is seeded per replicate. Output: one row per `(noise level, replicate)` plus
  the design count and best/worst recovery.
- `esm_embedding_linear_probe` — stratified k-fold logistic-regression probe on
  an ESM embedding matrix, with per-fold accuracy, mean/std accuracy and the
  majority-class baseline. Labels come from the `labels` parameter or from
  `info["labels"]` of the input form; features are standardised before the
  probe. Output: one `foldN` row per fold plus the three summary rows.
- `structure_zinc_sites` — finds zinc coordination sites and their ligands. When
  atom names are present, donors are restricted to cysteine `SG` and histidine
  `ND1`/`NE2`; otherwise sulfur/nitrogen atoms are used. Reports ligand count,
  mean/minimum zinc–donor distance and the per-ligand distances, and filters
  sites below `min_ligands`. Output: one row per site.
- `sequence_tm_segments` — transmembrane spans from a Kyte–Doolittle hydropathy
  profile (default 19-residue window, threshold 1.6). Consecutive hydrophobic
  windows are merged into one span, spans shorter than `min_length` are dropped.
  Output: one row per span with `start`, `end`, `length` and the mean/maximum
  hydropathy.

## Verification

- Hand-computed cases: the coordinate rewrite preserves every non-coordinate
  column and sigma 0 is a no-op; the separable 12×3 embedding matrix reaches
  accuracy 1.0 on three folds; the zinc fixture (three donors at 2.3/2.5/2.8 Å)
  yields mean 2.5333 Å with `LYS NZ` excluded when atom names exist and included
  in the element-only fallback; poly-leucine 30 resolves to a single span 1–30
  and a `L30-D10-L30` sequence to two ordered spans.
- Authoritative oracles: `sklearn.model_selection.cross_val_score` on the same
  pipeline and split reproduces the per-fold accuracies exactly; the noise
  operator is exercised against a fake `protein_mpnn_run.py` that records the
  forwarded `--pdb_path`, weights and `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES`, and the real
  runner is used on the Windows smoke.
- Focused suite `tests/test_extension233.py`–`236.py` passes (28 tests);
  protocol and reference-doc suites pass as well.
- Registry: 596 operators, 101 Readers, 7 packages, 24 routes, no errors.

## Compatibility

All four operators are new IDs with their own parameter schemas; no existing
operator, Reader, contract or saved workflow changes.
