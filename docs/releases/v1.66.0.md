# Operon 1.66.0

Seventh real-computation batch: a new **OpenMM** operator that builds a real
force field for a protein structure, reports the potential energy and
optionally minimizes it. The library grows to **655 operators and 101 Readers**.

## Added

- `structure_openmm_energy` (`StructureOpenmmEnergyStep`, category Structure,
  arity 1, output `[stage, statistic]`):
  - writes a fixed-column PDB from the `[atom, field]` Form (using the
    label-encoded `resname`/`chain`/`atom_name`/`element` columns);
  - repairs the structure with PDBFixer (missing atoms, non-standard residues,
    hydrogens at the configured pH);
  - builds an OpenMM system from `amber14-all.xml` + `implicit/gbn2.xml`
    (`NoCutoff`, `HBonds` constraints) on the CPU platform;
  - reports `potential_energy_kj` and `gradient_norm` for the `initial` and
    `minimized` stages, plus atom/particle/force counts and the energy delta in
    `info`.
  - Parameters: `atom_dim`, `field_dim`, `forcefield`, `implicit_model`, `ph`,
    `minimize`, `max_iterations`, `tolerance`, `stage_dim`, `output`.

## Evidence

- Real run on the 1MEL VHH chain A through the operator: 973 heavy atoms ->
  1881 particles, 6 force terms, 0 missing atoms, 1 missing terminal,
  70.6 s on CPU.
- Potential energy **2804.88 -> -15738.28 kJ/mol** (delta -18543.16); gradient
  norm **66341.7 -> 5857.1 kJ/mol/nm**.
- `pytest tests/test_extension295.py`: 4 passed, including the real minimization
  (49 s); the module skips cleanly when `openmm`/`pdbfixer` are absent.
- Registry 655/101/7/24 `errors []`; packaging gate passes.

## Boundaries

- Vacuum/implicit-solvent single-point and minimization only; no MD sampling,
  no explicit solvent, CPU platform only.
- PDBFixer may add or reorder atoms; the operator reports the repair counts so a
  caller can judge whether the input was complete.
