# Operon 1.42.0

Expansion batch with four capabilities covering helix curvature, halogen
bonding, design clustering and embedding layout.

## Capabilities

| Operator | Output | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `structure_helix_bend` | `[helix, statistic]`: n_residues, total_bend_deg, max_local_bend_deg, curvature_radius | Helices are detected from CA spacing; the axis bend is measured from windowed direction vectors and the curvature radius follows from the mean per-step turn. |
| `structure_halogen_bond_scan` | `[bond, statistic]`: distance, angle_deg | Carbon-halogen donors (F, Cl, Br, I with a carbon neighbour) against O/N/S acceptors using distance and C-X...Y angle criteria. |
| `proteinmpnn_design_cluster` | `[design, statistic]`: cluster, cluster_size, mean_identity, max_identity | Samples designs with the official runner, computes pairwise sequence identity and groups them by single-linkage clustering. |
| `esm_embedding_umap_projection` | `[sequence, component]` | Builds a binary kNN graph and projects the normalised Laplacian eigenvectors, giving a deterministic UMAP-style layout with fixed sign conventions. |

## Oracle evidence

- Helix bend: straight and curved analytic traces (a 20 Å arc recovers a
  curvature radius of 20 Å), window direction vectors checked directly.
- Halogen bonds: hand-computed 1.9 Å / 180° carbon-iodine...oxygen geometry,
  distance and angle filters, perpendicular and distant acceptors rejected.
- Design clustering: hand-sized identity matrix and consensus sequences, plus a
  fake official runner producing four designs with known identity structure.
- Spectral projection: hand adjacency for a kNN graph, Fiedler-vector sign split
  on two synthetic clusters, deterministic sign convention and shape checks.

## Limits and compatibility

- Helix bend uses CA geometry only; the curvature radius is an approximation for
  short or strongly bent helices.
- Halogen bonds use distance and angle criteria only; no bond-perception or
  charge information is inferred from the form.
- Design clustering needs the official ProteinMPNN checkout and model weights.
- The spectral projection is a Laplacian eigenmap, not UMAP itself; it keeps the
  input scale and does not learn a parametric mapping.
- No existing operator or Reader ids, contracts or parameters changed.
