# Operon 1.101.0

Two more trajectory-level capabilities (693 operators, 101 Readers):
**`structure_md_free_energy_landscape`** and
**`structure_md_conformational_clusters`**.

## Operators

### structure_md_free_energy_landscape

Projects CA fluctuations onto two principal components (reusing the PCA module),
histograms the projection and evaluates `F = -kT ln(p / p_max)` on the occupied
bins. Output rows carry PC1/PC2 bin indices, probability and free energy in
kJ/mol; info includes occupancy, temperature and the F range.

### structure_md_conformational_clusters

Computes the pairwise CA RMSD matrix (Kabsch superposition for every pair),
clusters frames with average linkage and a distance cutoff, and reports cluster
id and mean within-cluster distance per frame plus sizes and representative
frames. `scipy` is a declared dependency.

## Verification

- `tests/test_extension332.py`: 3 passed — analytic 2D Gaussian surface
  (`F ∝ r^2`, outer/inner ring ratio ≈ 4), invariance under a rigid rotation
  (sorted energies match to 1e-3), run round trip, duplicate-PC and
  non-positive-temperature errors.
- `tests/test_extension333.py`: 3 passed — three synthetic conformers cluster
  into exactly three groups, identical frames into one, run round trip and
  cutoff validation.
- Real OpenMM trajectory (1MEL chain A, 20 frames): free-energy surface over 15
  occupied bins, F range 0-2.74 kJ/mol, probabilities sum to 1.0; at a 1.5 A
  cutoff all 20 frames form one cluster (max pairwise RMSD 1.08 A), consistent
  with a short rigid trajectory.
- Registry: 693 operators / 101 Readers / 7 packages / 24 routes, `errors []`.

## Boundaries

- Free energies are histogram estimates; with 20 frames only 15 of 144 bins are
  populated. Meaningful landscapes need hundreds of frames.
- Clustering cost is O(frames^2) superpositions; the operators are intended for
  hundreds, not millions, of frames.
- Clustering is on CA atoms only.
