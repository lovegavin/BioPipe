# Operon backend 1.22.0

Backend release on the 1.x line. SDK remains 3.1.0, the operator protocol
remains v3 and the library grows to **488 operators / 101 Readers**, seven
providers and 24 route templates. This is an additive expansion: no operator
or Reader was renamed, removed or re-versioned.

## New capabilities

| Capability | Kind | Provider | Reference implementation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `esm_variant_effect` | operator | `operon-core` | fair-esm `ESM2` masked-marginal scoring |
| `proteinmpnn_interface_design` | operator | `operon-core` | official ProteinMPNN `protein_mpnn_run.py` with fixed/bias/tied positions |
| `rdkit_admet_rules` | operator | `operon-core` | RDKit Lipinski, Veber and Ghose rule sets |
| `rdkit_conformer_energy` | operator | `operon-core` | RDKit ETKDGv3 embedding + MMFF94/UFF minimization |
| `antibody_cdr_kabat` | operator | `operon-core` | ANARCI/Kabat CDR definitions (in-house hybrid implementation) |

The registry counts move from 483 operators / 101 Readers to **488 operators /
101 Readers**. No Reader is added in this release.

### `esm_variant_effect`

Zero-shot amino-acid substitution scanning. Each requested position is masked
and the operator reports the log-likelihood ratio of every alternative residue
against the wild type (`llr`, `wt_log_prob`, `mut_log_prob`, `mutant_rank`) in a
`[mutation, statistic]` Form. Substitutions are ordered by rank within each
position, `info.top_mutations` keeps the strongest ten overall and positions are
processing in batches to bound memory. The probability is read from the
un-renormalized model vocabulary, so the twenty standard-amino-acid
probabilities of a position sum to at most one.

### `proteinmpnn_interface_design`

Runs the official ProteinMPNN runner on a fixed backbone with an explicit binder
and target split. Every target residue is emitted in the fixed-positions JSONL
(for every chain, including empty design-only chains), binder residues can be
selected with `design_ranges`; optional amino-acid biases, omitted residues and
tied positions map to the official `--bias_AA_jsonl`, `--omit_AAs` and
`--tied_positions_jsonl` inputs. Designs are returned as `[design, statistic]`
with `score`, `global_score` and `seq_recovery`, and the sequences are kept in
`info.sequences`. The operator rejects design/fixed overlaps, chains that are
not in the structure, non-finite scores and malformed bias or tie expressions.

### `rdkit_admet_rules`

Computes the Lipinski, Veber and Ghose descriptors for each input molecule plus
the rule verdicts: `lipinski_violations`, `veber_pass` and `ghose_pass`.
Threshold parameters (molecular weight, logP, donors, acceptors, TPSA,
rotatable bonds) can be tightened per workflow, and the chosen thresholds are
echoed in `info.thresholds`.

### `rdkit_conformer_energy`

Enumerates conformers with ETKDGv3 and optionally minimizes them with MMFF94,
MMFF94s or UFF. Returns `n_conformers`, `min_energy`, `mean_energy`,
`energy_spread` and a `minimized` flag in a `[molecule, statistic]` Form; the
per-conformer energies are kept in `info.conformer_energies` in kcal/mol.
Embedding is seeded so repeated runs are identical.

### `antibody_cdr_kabat`

Annotates the seven Kabat-style regions (FR1, CDR1, FR2, CDR2, FR3, CDR3, FR4)
of a single VH/VHH sequence. CDR-H1 is derived from the conserved Cys22/Trp
anchors, CDR-H2 from a global alignment to an embedded Kabat-numbered VHH
template restricted to the CDR2 anchor, and CDR-H3 from the first cysteine after
FR3 up to the conserved WGQG/WGKG framework-4 motif, so C-terminal tags and
internal cysteines do not shift the call. The regions are returned in a
`[region, statistic]` Form (`start`, `end`, `length`), with `info.region_sequences`
and `info.cdr_sequences` for downstream use. A template identity gate rejects
sequences that are not immunoglobulin domains.

## Limits

- `esm_variant_effect` and `esm_pseudo_likelihood` require a local ESM2
  checkpoint; the operators never download weights.
- `proteinmpnn_interface_design` requires a local ProteinMPNN checkout and the
  official weights, and the PDB must carry N, CA, C and O backbone atoms for
  every designed residue (CA-only structures yield non-finite scores, which the
  operator rejects explicitly).
- `antibody_cdr_kabat` is a Kabat-style hybrid, not a full HMM-based numbering
  of every framework position; its accuracy is validated on VHH fixtures only.
- `rdkit_conformer_energy` energies come from RDKit force fields and are not
  comparable across force fields or to quantum-chemistry references.

## Compatibility

No runtime, schema or parameter semantics changed for existing operators. Saved
workflows keep their frozen parameter values; the five new capabilities are new
IDs and do not re-pin existing graphs.
