# Operon 1.74.0

Fifteenth real-computation batch: **ligand geometry and protein-clash quality
checks**. The library grows to **663 operators and 101 Readers**.

## Added

- `structure_ligand_geometry_qc` (`StructureLigandGeometryQcStep`, category
  Structure, arity 1, output `[ligand, statistic]`):
  - selects ligand rows by residue name, renders a PDB block and lets RDKit
    perceive bonds and sanitize the molecule;
  - flags bonds whose measured length falls outside `0.6-1.3 x` the sum of
    covalent radii by more than `bond_tolerance`;
  - counts ligand-protein heavy-atom clashes below `clash_cutoff` and reports
    the minimum protein distance;
  - outputs `n_atoms`, `n_bonds`, `n_bond_outliers`, `max_bond_deviation`,
    `n_clashes`, `min_protein_distance`, with outlier bond details in info.

## Evidence

- Aspirin (`CC(=O)Oc1ccccc1C(=O)O`) from an RDKit MMFF conformer: 21 atoms,
  19-21 perceived bonds, **0 bond outliers**, 0 clashes.
- The same ligand with a synthetic protein atom 1.2 A away: **3 clashes**,
  minimum distance **1.2 A**, geometry still clean.
- `pytest tests/test_extension303.py`: 5 passed (ideal geometry, stretched bond,
  clash summary, runner contract, missing-ligand error).

## Boundaries

- Connectivity is perceived from the PDB block, so a severely distorted bond can
  disappear from the perceived graph rather than be flagged; a template-SMILES
  bond-order assignment is the planned refinement.
- Geometry bounds use covalent radii with a wide 0.6-1.3 window: this is a gross
  geometry sanity check, not a strain-energy or quantum-chemistry validation.
- Only heavy-atom clashes are counted; hydrogens are ignored on the protein side.
