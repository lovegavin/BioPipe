# Operon v1.16.0

Released 2026-11-01. Backend/application version **1.16.0**; operator library
version **1.16.0**; SDK **3.1.0**; protocol **v3**.

This batch adds four capabilities: bio-environment matching, the Morisita
aggregation index, and NIfTI/NRRD volume readers. The registry grows to
**464 operators / 96 Readers / 7 packages / 24 routes**.

## Added capabilities

| Capability | Kind | Provider | Contract |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `bioenv` | Operator | `operon-microbiome` | community `[sample, taxon]` × environment `[sample, env]` |
| `morisita` | Operator | `operon-microbiome` | abundance `[sample, feature]` |
| `NiftiReader` | Reader | `operon-readers` | `.nii` → `[slice, voxel]` |
| `NrrdReader` | Reader | `operon-readers` | `.nrrd` → `[slice, voxel]` |

### bioenv

`bioenv` selects environmental variable subsets of every size from 1 to
`upto`, standardizes the subset by variable, and reports the largest
Spearman or Pearson correlation between community Bray-Curtis distances and
Euclidean distances of the scaled variables. Output is one `rho` per subset
size; `info` records every model, the best size, the best variables and the
constant columns dropped. `partial` matching is not implemented.

### morisita

`morisita` computes the Morisita aggregation index (`imor`), the clumping and
uniformity thresholds (`mclu`, `muni`), the standardized index (`imst`) and
the chi-square probability (`pchisq`) per species. Features with `rs <= 1`
produce NaN rows recorded in `info.skipped`; `unique_rm` drops species present
in a single sample before the calculation.

### NiftiReader

`NiftiReader` parses NIfTI-1 headers directly (no nibabel at runtime): it
detects endianness from `sizeof_hdr`, rejects non-single-file magic, maps the
common numeric datatype codes, applies `scl_slope`/`scl_inter`, and prefers the
sform affine over the quaternion form. Slices are the third axis; every slice
is flattened over the first two axes into `[slice, voxel]`. `.nii.gz` is
handled by the reader's automatic decompression wrapper.

### NrrdReader

`NrrdReader` parses the textual NRRD header (`NRRD000x`), supports raw and
gzip encodings and the `line skip`/`byte skip` offsets, and maps the numerics
listed in the format. Dimensions 2 and 3 are accepted; volumetric data is
flattened to `[slice, voxel]` with the fastest-varying size first, as NRRD
stores it.

## Authoritative comparison

- `bioenv` and `morisita` are checked against R 4.x (`vegan 2.7.6`) with
  `Rscript`; correlations and all five Morisita columns agree to the tested
  tolerances.
- `NiftiReader` is compared with `nibabel 5.4.2` on a written int16 volume
  with slope/inter; data, voxel size, affine and scaling metadata agree.
- `NrrdReader` is compared with `pynrrd 1.1.3` for gzip-encoded volumes and
  hand-written big-endian 2-D raw files.

## Limits

- `bioenv` uses only the default full-subset search (`upto` bounded by the
  number of explanatory variables); `partial` matching and non-Euclidean
  environmental metrics are not implemented.
- `NiftiReader` accepts only the single-file NIfTI-1 layout, not the
  two-file `.hdr`/`.img` pair, and rejects `ndim > 3`.
- `NrrdReader` does not follow detached `data file` headers, and accepts
  only 2-D/3-D volumes.
- NIfTI/NRRD readers need the byte order declared by their formats; complex
  datatypes are not supported.

## Compatibility

Workflows that do not use the new operators or readers are unaffected. The
SDK contract and protocol version are unchanged; saved workflow parameters
keep their values.
