# Operon 1.31.0

Backend release **1.31.0** (minor: adds capabilities). Registry: **530 operators,
101 Readers, seven packages, 24 routes**.

## New capabilities

| ID | Kind | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `proteinmpnn_position_entropy` | operator | Per-position sequence entropy and recovery of ProteinMPNN designs |
| `esm_embedding_pca` | operator | Principal-component projection of an ESM embedding form |
| `esm_embedding_neighbors` | operator | Cosine/euclidean nearest neighbours in embedding space |
| `structure_disulfide_bonds` | operator | Disulfide-bond pairs from cysteine SG atoms |
| `structure_salt_bridges` | operator | Salt bridges between charged residue centroids |

## Design notes

- `proteinmpnn_position_entropy` runs the official `protein_mpnn_run.py` sampling
  interface, parses `<out>/seqs/<stem>.fa`, drops the native metadata record and
  computes the base-2 entropy `-sum(p log2 p)` over the twenty standard amino
  acids, together with per-position recovery, mode frequency and observation
  count. At least two generated designs are required for the entropy estimate.
- `esm_embedding_pca` centres the embedding matrix and projects it with a
  singular-value decomposition, so it does not require scikit-learn at runtime;
  the report includes explained-variance ratios, singular values and loadings.
- `esm_embedding_neighbors` ranks the other sequences for every sequence using
  cosine similarity (chosen as the default because ESM embeddings are typically
  compared by direction) or euclidean distance, and returns the neighbour index
  matrix with row labels preserved.
- `structure_disulfide_bonds` selects `SG` atoms of `CYS` residues and reports
  every pair within `max_distance` (default 2.5 A); bonded and free cysteines are
  listed in `info`.
- `structure_salt_bridges` averages the charged atoms of ASP/GLU (anionic) and
  LYS/ARG/HIS (cationic) into residue centroids and reports pairs within
  `max_distance` (default 4.0 A), splitting counts into intra- and inter-chain
  bridges.

## Authoritative checks

- ProteinMPNN entropy: the gated test copies the local official checkout
  `~/.operon/resources/proteinmpnn`, runs a real sampling pass on the tiny backbone
  fixture, and checks the 16-position output. A hand-computed table (four
  designs, one variable position) verifies the entropy arithmetic exactly.
- ESM-IF/embedding: PCA is compared against an independent SVD and scikit-learn
  implementation, neighbours against scikit-learn `pairwise_distances` and a
  hand cosine matrix.
- Structures: disulfides are compared with a Biopython `NeighborSearch` oracle;
  salt bridges against hand-computed centroids and distances.
- Runner-level tests (tests/test_extension169.py) pass every operator through
  `src.task.runner.run` and the real `Context` output-contract check.

## Limits

- `proteinmpnn_position_entropy` needs a local ProteinMPNN checkout and weights;
  the estimate is sample-based (entropy is biased for small `num_seq_per_target`).
- PCA components and neighbour counts are capped at 50.
- `structure_salt_bridges` models charges by geometry only: it ignores pKa
  shifts, buried side chains and explicit hydrogens, and HIS is always counted
  as cationic.
- `structure_disulfide_bonds` requires `SG` atoms in the PDB form; structures
  without cysteine SG atoms raise rather than returning an empty table.

## Compatibility

The five operators are additive. Existing workflows, pins and parameter values
are unchanged; no Reader contract changed in this release.
