# Operon 1.76.0

Seventeenth real-computation batch: **CAPRI DockQ complex quality scoring**.
The library grows to **665 operators and 101 Readers**.

## Added

- `structure_dockq` (`StructureDockqStep`, category Structure, arity 1, output
  `[complex, statistic]`):
  - decodes model and native atom tables, splits receptor and ligand chains,
    counts native residue contacts within 5 A and the recovered fraction;
  - defines interface residues within 10 A in the native, superposes their
    backbone atoms for iRMSD, and superposes the receptor to measure the ligand
    backbone LRMSD;
  - reports `fnat`, `irmsd`, `lrmsd`, `dockq`, with native contact and interface
    residue counts in info.

## Evidence (oracle: official `DockQ` CLI)

- 1MEL A/L, identical complex: ours `dockq 1.000` (fnat 1.0, iRMSD 0, LRMSD 0).
- Chains A/B shifted by (+2, 0, +1) A: ours `fnat 0.750, iRMSD 0.858, LRMSD
  2.150, dockq 0.8145`; official AL pair `fnat 0.716, iRMSD 0.843, LRMSD 2.236,
  DockQ 0.8040`; difference 0.0105.
- `pytest tests/test_extension305.py`: 4 passed, including the CLI comparison.

## Boundaries

- Contacts are resolved at residue level after heavy-atom neighbour search; the
  official tool resolves chain mappings automatically and averages over them, so
  multi-chain complexes can differ by a few hundredths.
- No chain-permutation search: the caller declares receptor and ligand chains.
- Small-molecule and peptide CAPRI variants are not implemented.
