# Operon backend 1.19.0

Backend release on the 1.x line. SDK remains 3.1.0 and the operator protocol
remains v3; this is an additive expansion that adds a sequence-design and
structure-interface layer, so existing workflows and frozen parameter values
keep working.

## New capabilities

| Capability | Kind | Provider | Reference implementation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `proteinmpnn_design` | operator | `operon-core` | ProteinMPNN `protein_mpnn_run.py` |
| `esm_masked_marginal` | operator | `operon-core` | fair-esm `load_model_and_alphabet_local` |
| `minibinder_interface` | operator | `operon-core` | in-house contact geometry |
| `ligand_contact_profile` | operator | `operon-core` | in-house contact geometry |
| `FastaReader` (additive) | Reader | `operon-readers` | protein alphabet support |
| `PdbReader` (additive) | Reader | `operon-readers` | full-atom PDB columns |

Registry counts move from 470 operators / 100 Readers to **474 operators /
100 Readers**, seven providers and 24 route templates. The two Reader changes
are additive extensions of existing IDs (both keep protocol version 1.0.0),
not new Readers.

### `proteinmpnn_design`

Runs the official ProteinMPNN command-line entry point through `subprocess` and
parses the generated FASTA records back into a `[design, statistic]` Form.
Parameters cover the structure (`structure_path`), the local checkout and
weights (`proteinmpnn_path`, `weights_path`, `model_name`), sampling
(`num_seq_per_target`, `sampling_temp`, `seed`), residue selection
(`chains`, `fixed_positions`, `design_ranges`) and execution (`device`,
`python_executable`, `timeout_seconds`). `design_ranges` accepts ranges such as
`A:97-110,A:26-33`, which is the supported way to restrict redesign to CDR
loops. The output carries `score`, `global_score` and `seq_recovery` per design
and the designed sequences in `info.sequences`.

### `esm_masked_marginal`

Computes ESM masked-marginal probabilities for point mutations of a protein
FASTA form (`mutations` as `A12V,C34W`). The 20 standard amino acids are
normalised with a max-shifted softmax over the masked position's logits; the
output is a `[mutation, statistic]` Form with `wt_prob`, `mut_prob` and
`log_ratio`, plus `info.masked_positions`. Model loading is lazy and uses
`esm.pretrained.load_model_and_alphabet_local`, so no weights are downloaded.

### `minibinder_interface`

Consumes a `PdbReader` `[atom, field]` Form and reports interface residues
between binder and target chains: `contact_count` and `min_distance` per
residue. `binder_chains` and `target_chains` accept comma-separated lists and
either side may be derived from the other. Water and other solvent residues are
excluded through `exclude_resnames`, hydrogen atoms through the element/atom
name check, and residues below `min_contacts` are dropped.

### `ligand_contact_profile`

Consumes the same PDB Form and reports, for every ligand heavy atom,
`contact_count` and `min_distance` against receptor heavy atoms within
`cutoff`, with the nearest residue recorded in `info.nearest_residue` and the
per-atom contact residues in `info.contact_residues`.

### Readers

`FastaReader` now understands protein sequences. The new `alphabet` parameter
accepts `auto`, `dna`, `rna` or `protein`; `protein` maps the 20 standard
residues plus `*` to codes 0-20 and uses 21 as the unknown sentinel, while
nucleotide inputs keep the existing 0-5 coding. `auto` infers the alphabet from
the observed characters, so protein FASTA files no longer collapse to the
unknown code.

`PdbReader` additionally exposes `atom_name`, `altloc` and `insertion_code`,
appended after the existing nine columns, and reports the column list in
`info.fields`. Column addressing by label is unchanged.

## Evidence

- `proteinmpnn_design`: a fake ProteinMPNN checkout records the exact argv,
  working directory, environment and fixed-position JSONL written by the step;
  the generated FASTA is parsed back byte-for-byte. A gated test runs the real
  official checkout with the local `v_48_020` weights on CPU and validates the
  parsed scores and sequence length.
- `esm_masked_marginal`: the masked-marginal maths is compared value-by-value
  against a hand-computed softmax over deterministic logits from a fake ESM
  module; mutation parsing and wild-type validation have explicit failure
  tests.
- `minibinder_interface` / `ligand_contact_profile`: a nine-atom, multi-chain
  fixture is ranked by hand (contact counts and Euclidean distances) and
  compared exactly; error paths cover missing chains, missing heavy atoms and
  empty interfaces.
- `FastaReader` / `PdbReader`: protein codes are compared against
  `_sequence_common.PROTEIN_MAP`, nucleotide codes against the legacy map, and
  PDB coordinates/residues against Biopython's `PDBParser`.
- `tests/test_extension110.py` to `tests/test_extension113.py` contain 26 tests
  (one gated real-inference test), all passing.

## Limits

- ProteinMPNN is not bundled: the operator requires a local checkout and local
  weights. Only the small `v_48_*` weights are exercised in tests.
- `esm_masked_marginal` requires local ESM weights and `fair-esm`; neither is
  downloaded automatically.
- `proteinmpnn_design` reports per-design scores and sequences; per-residue
  probability matrices (`--save_probs`) are not exposed.
- `minibinder_interface` derives the target side from all non-excluded chains,
  so ligand or other HETATM chains should be named explicitly in
  `target_chains` when they must stay out.
- `PdbReader` still exposes no full PDB reconstruction: the new columns are
  read-only descriptors.

## Verification

Focused tests (`test_extension8`, `test_assets`, `test_extension110`-`113`,
`test_protocol_v2`, `test_reference_docs`) pass, `scripts/build_operator_docs.py`
regenerates cleanly, and `operon registry check` reports 474 operators,
100 Readers, seven packages and 24 routes with no errors.

## Compatibility

- SDK 3.1.0, operator protocol v3.
- No operator or Reader IDs were changed or removed.
- `FastaReader` reading a protein FASTA with the default `alphabet` now yields
  protein codes instead of the unknown sentinel; dims, labels and missing-value
  handling are unchanged.
- `PdbReader` appends three columns; consumers that address fields by label are
  unaffected, while code relying on the literal column count must use
  `info.fields`.
