# Operon 1.36.0

Released 2026-11-02. Expansion batch: dihedral secondary-structure segments,
absolute and relative contact order, BRICS fragment overlap matrices, and
ProteinMPNN design-diversity clustering.

Backend application version **1.36.0**, protocol version 3, SDK 3.1.0.
Generated indexes are **551 operators**, **101 Readers**, seven packages and
24 built-in routes.

## New capabilities

| Operator | Category | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `structure_secondary_structure` | Machine Learning | Assigns helix (H), strand (E) and coil (C) from backbone phi/psi dihedrals with a smoothing window and reports contiguous segments. |
| `structure_contact_order` | Machine Learning | CA-CA contact order: per-residue contact counts and mean sequence separation, plus absolute and relative contact order. |
| `rdkit_fragment_overlap` | Machine Learning | Jaccard overlap of BRICS fragment sets between two molecule groups (`SMILES [name]` lines), as a molecule_a x molecule_b matrix. |
| `proteinmpnn_design_diversity` | Machine Learning | Runs the official ProteinMPNN runner and reports per-design mean/min/nearest identity and single-linkage clusters at an identity threshold. |

## Design notes

- `structure_secondary_structure` keeps `phi`/`psi` computation independent of
  DSSP (only the three-state comparison is cross-checked against `mkdssp`), and
  demotes helical/strand runs shorter than the smoothing window to coil.
- `structure_contact_order` follows the standard definitions: absolute contact
  order is the mean sequence separation divided by chain length, relative
  contact order is the mean sequence separation itself.
- `rdkit_fragment_overlap` strips BRICS dummy atoms before the Jaccard
  comparison and falls back to the canonical SMILES when no fragment survives
  the minimum size.
- `proteinmpnn_design_diversity` uses the official runner (sequential 1-based
  chain indices internally) and computes all identity statistics in Python, so
  no external dependency beyond numpy is required.

## Limits

- The three-state assignment is dihedral-based and does not reproduce DSSP's
  hydrogen-bond patterns; use it for coarse segments, not for exact DSSP
  equivalence.
- Contact order is computed over CA atoms of a single chain; multi-chain
  complexes need one chain at a time.
- Design diversity depends on the local ProteinMPNN checkout and weights and
  costs one full sampling run per invocation.

## Compatibility

Additive capabilities only. Existing operator ids, ports and saved parameter
values are unchanged; the registry count grows from 547 to 551 operators while
Readers, packages and routes stay at 101 / 7 / 24.
