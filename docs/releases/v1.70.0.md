# Operon 1.70.0

Eleventh real-computation batch: **short molecular dynamics with drift
statistics** through OpenMM. The library grows to **659 operators and 101
Readers**.

## Added

- `structure_openmm_md_stability` (`StructureOpenmmMdStabilityStep`, category
  Structure, arity 1, output `[frame, statistic]`):
  - strips waters, repairs the structure with PDBFixer (`--ph` protonation),
    builds an Amber14 + GBN2 implicit-solvent system with `HBonds` constraints,
    minimises, assigns Maxwell-Boltzmann velocities, equilibrates for
    `equilibrate_steps`, then runs `steps` production steps with a seeded
    `LangevinMiddleIntegrator`.
  - samples every `sample_every` steps and reports `time_ps`, `temperature_k`
    (from the kinetic energy and the constraint-corrected degrees of freedom),
    `potential_energy_kj`, `rmsd_angstrom` (Kabsch to the minimised structure)
    and `radius_of_gyration_angstrom`.
  - Parameters: `atom_dim`, `field_dim`, `remove_water`, `ph`, `temperature`,
    `friction`, `step_size_fs`, `steps`, `sample_every`, `minimize_iterations`,
    `equilibrate_steps`, `seed`, `forcefield`, `frame_dim`, `output`.

## Evidence

- OpenMM 8.6.1 + PDBFixer, VHH chain A: **1881 particles, 907 constraints**,
  minimised potential energy about -11 260 kJ/mol.
- 500 equilibration + 1000 production steps (2 ps at 2 fs): temperature
  **250 → 295 K**, RMSD **≈1.0 Å**, radius of gyration **14.26 Å** with 0.01 Å
  drift, 162 s on the CPU platform.
- `pytest tests/test_extension299.py`: 3 passed, 1 skipped (the gated real
  trajectory is skipped without `OPERON_OPENMM_REAL=1`).

## Boundaries

- Microsecond-scale MD is out of scope: this is a stability/drift probe for
  small systems, not a production simulation.
- CPU-platform reduction order makes repeated runs differ slightly even with a
  fixed seed (temperature ~280 K mean vs the 300 K target); rankings and
  geometry drift remain comparable, absolute values are not bitwise stable.
- Implicit solvent only (GBN2); no explicit water, no pressure coupling, no
  free-energy perturbation.
