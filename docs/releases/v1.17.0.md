# Operon 1.17.0

Backend release: five new capabilities — three statistical/unsupervised operators and
two three-dimensional mesh readers.

## Capabilities

| Component | Kind | Provider | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `nelson_aalen` | operator | `operon-core` | Nelson-Aalen cumulative hazard from a time/event table |
| `gower_distance` | operator | `operon-core` | Gower distance for mixed numeric and categorical data |
| `pam` | operator | `operon-core` | Partitioning around medoids (BUILD + exact SWAP) |
| `StlReader` | reader | `operon-readers` | Binary and ASCII STL triangle meshes |
| `ObjReader` | reader | `operon-readers` | Wavefront OBJ vertices and polygon faces |

Registry after the batch: **467 operators, 98 Readers, 7 providers, 24 routes**.

## Evidence

- `nelson_aalen` matches `survival::survfit(..., type = "fh")` on the shared
  fixture: times `2,3,5,6,8,10,12`, cumulative hazard
  `0.125,0.125,0.4916667,0.4916667,0.825,0.825,1.825` and standard errors
  `0.125,0.125,0.2887954,0.2887954,0.4410373,0.4410373,1.0929382`. Tied events
  contribute one increment per event, `1/n + 1/(n-1) + ...`.
- `gower_distance` matches `cluster::daisy(metric = "gower")` on a mixed
  numeric/categorical fixture, including per-column range normalisation,
  categorical mismatch, zero-range columns and missing-value reweighting.
- `pam` reproduces `cluster::pam(dist, 2)` and `cluster::pam(dist, 3)` medoids,
  cluster assignments, build objective and final objective on the shared fixture
  (`id.med 2,6` and `2,6,8`, objective `1.5170912829` and `0.4651732643`).
- `StlReader` binary and ASCII output agrees with `trimesh` on vertices and
  triangle sets; `ObjReader` agrees with `trimesh` on the tetra fixture and
  handles negative indices and polygon fan triangulation.
- Tests: `tests/test_extension102.py` (operators), `tests/test_extension16_readers.py`
  (readers), `tests/test_r_oracles98.py` (R oracle).

## Limits

- `nelson_aalen` reports the cumulative hazard and its standard error only; it does
  not produce confidence bands, survival curves or log-rank tests.
- `gower_distance` requires categorical columns to be named explicitly and treats
  numeric columns with range normalisation; it does not support weighted columns.
- `pam` uses Euclidean distance, requires `2 <= n_clusters < n_samples`, and runs a
  single BUILD/SWAP pass; it does not compute silhouettes or multiple starts.
- `StlReader` reads triangle geometry only (no colours or materials) and merges
  vertices by rounded coordinates; `ObjReader` keeps vertex positions and faces,
  ignoring texture coordinates, normals and materials; polygons are split by a
  deterministic fan.

## Compatibility

Backend-only release: the SDK stays at 3.1.0 and the operator protocol stays at v3.
Existing workflows are unaffected; the new components are additive.
