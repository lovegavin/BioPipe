# v1.39.0 — surface hydrophobicity, ORF finding, atom environments, metal sites and CDR lengths

Backend 1.39.0 expands the library to 566 operators and 101 Readers. The batch
adds three structure operators, one nucleotide operator and one molecule
operator, each with a compiled parameter schema, generated Markdown page and a
focused test module.

## Capabilities

| ID | Kind | Input | Output | Oracle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `structure_surface_hydrophobicity` | structure | PDB `[atom, field]` | `[residue, statistic]` | isolated-sphere area `4π(r+probe)²` |
| `sequence_orf_finder` | nucleotide | `[sequence, position]` | `[orf, statistic]` | hand-built six-frame constructs |
| `rdkit_atom_environment_histogram` | molecule | none (SMILES parameter) | `[molecule, statistic]` | `GetMorganFingerprint` counts |
| `structure_metal_coordination` | structure | PDB `[atom, field]` | `[metal, statistic]` | hand coordination shells |
| `antibody_cdr_length_profile` | antibody | `[sequence, position]` | `[sequence, statistic]` | ANARCI-derived Kabat CDRs on 1MEL/5VNV |

### structure_surface_hydrophobicity

Multiplies the Shrake–Rupley accessible area of each residue (heavy atoms,
Bondi radii, configurable probe radius and sphere density) by the Kyte–Doolittle
scale. Columns are `sasa`, `sasa_fraction`, `hydrophobicity` and
`hydrophobic_sasa`; `info` reports the total and hydrophobic area and the
hydrophobic surface fraction. Rows are ordered by hydrophobic exposure so the
most hydrophobic patch is first.

### sequence_orf_finder

Scans all six frames of every nucleotide sequence with a lookahead on
`ATG`/`TAA`/`TAG`/`TGA`, reporting frame, 1-based start/end, length and
start/stop flags. `require_start` can be disabled to report any open frame.
`info.orfs` carries the coding nucleotides of every hit. The operator rejects
protein-encoded forms instead of silently translating them.

### rdkit_atom_environment_histogram

Counts hashed Morgan atom environments at a configurable radius
(1–4) with `useCounts`, and derives the number of environments, unique
environments, the most frequent environment and Shannon entropy. Raw counts are
kept in `info.environments` for downstream circular fingerprints.

### structure_metal_coordination

Detects metal ions by element or residue name (Zn, Mg, Mn, Fe, Ca, Cu, Co, Ni,
Na, K, Cd, Hg), builds a KD-tree over heavy ligand atoms and reports the
coordination number, mean distance and the oxygen/nitrogen/sulfur split within a
distance cutoff. `info.shells` lists the coordinating atom labels and distances.

### antibody_cdr_length_profile

Locates the Kabat CDRs with the existing anchor-based annotator and reports
CDR1/2/3 lengths, the total CDR length, the CDR3 hydrophobic fraction and the
CDR3 net charge. Both `sequence` and `position` dimensions are validated, and
`info.sequences` stores the region sequences for downstream QC.

  50 passed, 2 skipped. Packaging group: 65 passed.

## Limits

- The hydrophobicity scale covers the 20 standard residues; modified residues
  score zero and are documented as such.
- The ORF finder is nucleotide-only and uses the standard genetic code; it does
  not model mitochondrial or non-standard codes.
- Metal coordination uses element/residue heuristics and a hard cutoff; it does
  not infer coordination geometry (octahedral/square-planar).
- CDR annotation inherits the hybrid Kabat approach of `antibody_cdr_kabat`
  (anchor rules plus a global template alignment) and is tuned for VH/VHH.

## Compatibility

No existing operator changes behaviour. The registry grows to 566 operators and
101 Readers; pinned workflows keep their frozen parameter values.
