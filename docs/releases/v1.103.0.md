# Operon 1.103.0

One hard capability (695 operators, 101 Readers):
**`structure_md_hbond_lifetime`** — hydrogen-bond residence times.

## Operator

Reuses the geometry definitions of the occupancy operator (donor-H pairing by
nearest heavy donor, H···A <= `max_distance`, D-H···A >= `min_angle`) and runs
them across every frame. For each pair above `min_occupancy` it reports
occupancy, mean and maximum continuous lifetime in frames, the number of
continuous runs, state transitions, the lifetime converted to picoseconds
(`frame_time_ps`) and the mean H···A distance. This is kinetics, not just
occupancy: a bond that flickers on and off has many transitions and a short
mean lifetime even at high occupancy.

## Verification

  (`[T,T,F,T,T,T,F,T] -> [2,3,1]`), a hand case with occupancy 0.8, mean
  lifetime 4 frames, one transition, 2.0 ps at `frame_time_ps=0.5`, plus a real
  operator round trip on a three-atom PDB with a persistent H bond
  (occupancy 1.0, 3.0 ps).
- Real OpenMM trajectory (1MEL chain A, 1881 particles, 20 frames, 10 k
  candidate H bonds): 5 pairs above 0.5 occupancy, maximum lifetime **4.0 ps**
  (a bond present in every frame), maximum occupancy 1.0.
- Registry: 695 operators / 101 Readers / 7 packages / 24 routes, `errors []`.

## Boundaries

- Life times are sampled at the frame interval; bonds shorter than one frame
  cannot be resolved, and with 20 frames the statistics are coarse.
- The detector uses geometric criteria only (no QM/MM or proton-transfer
  modelling), and waters are not considered.
- The donor-H assignment assumes hydrogens are present in the topology.
