# Operon 1.107.0

One hard capability (699 operators, 101 Readers):
**`structure_md_tica`** — time-lagged independent component analysis of a
trajectory.

## Operator

Superposes CA frames, optionally pre-reduces with PCA (`n_pca`, 10 by default),
builds the instantaneous and lagged covariance matrices from centred
coordinates, symmetrises `C(tau)`, whitens with a regularised `C(0)` and solves
`C(tau) v = lambda C(0) v`. Output rows are `ic1..icN` with eigenvalue and
implied timescale `-lag * frame_time / ln(lambda)`; info carries the leading
eigenvalues, the timescale list and the slowest timescale.

## Verification

- `tests/test_extension339.py`: 3 passed — AR(1) data with coefficient 0.9
  recovers the TICA eigenvalue to 0.02; the analytic timescale relation is
  checked to 1e-12; and a full operator round trip on 40 synthetic frames
  produces a row-stochastic-shaped spectrum and a positive slow timescale.
- Real OpenMM trajectory (1MEL chain A, 20 frames, `n_pca=6`): at lag 1 the
  leading eigenvalues are **0.995 / 0.937 / 0.845** with implied timescales
  **36.84 / 3.06 / 1.19 ps**; at lag 5 the spectrum drops to 0.443 / 0.319,
  showing the expected lag dependence.
- Registry: 699 operators / 101 Readers / 7 packages / 24 routes, `errors []`.

## Boundaries

- The estimator is a finite-sample one: with 20 frames eigenvalues can slightly
  exceed 1 (seen at lag 10, 1.04) and those modes yield no physical timescale;
  longer trajectories are required for reliable kinetics.
- PCA pre-reduction and whitening regularisation are exposed as parameters
  (`n_pca`, `regularization`) because they materially change the spectrum.
- Only CA coordinates are used; no dihedral or contact features.
