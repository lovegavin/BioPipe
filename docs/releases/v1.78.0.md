# Operon 1.78.0

Nineteenth real-computation batch: **short molecular dynamics with per-residue
fluctuations**. The library grows to **667 operators and 101 Readers**.

## Added

- `structure_openmm_md_rmsf` (`StructureOpenmmMdRmsfStep`, category Structure,
  arity 1, output `[residue, statistic]`):
  - repairs the input with PDBFixer (missing residues, non-standard residues,
    hydrogens at the selected pH), keeps only the requested chains, and builds
    an Amber14 + GB-Neck2 system with `NoCutoff` and `HBonds` constraints;
  - minimises for 100 iterations and runs Langevin dynamics at the requested
    temperature with a 2 fs step, collecting C-alpha frames every
    `report_interval` steps;
  - aligns each frame to the first frame with Kabsch and reports per-residue
    RMSF around the ensemble mean, plus the radius of gyration statistics;
  - exposes the trajectory (`.npy`), the prepared structure and the C-alpha
    indices in info for independent re-analysis.

## Evidence (oracle: mdtraj)

- Oracle test on a real 200-step run: our RMSF agrees with `mdtraj.rmsf` to
  **1.6e-7 nm** on the same trajectory.
- Real run (1MEL chain A, 2000 steps = 4 ps, 20 frames, CPU): **156 s**, mean
  RMSF **0.0477 nm**, range 0.0241-0.1131 nm, radius of gyration 1.398-1.417 nm
  (mean 1.408). The top flexible residues are PRO41 (0.113 nm) and GLY42
  (0.111 nm) in the CDR-H2 loop, then SER133 at the C-terminus.
- `pytest tests/test_extension307.py`: 4 passed.

## Boundaries

- Implicit solvent only, no periodic box, no pressure coupling; 4 ps is far
  below equilibration time, so absolute RMSF values are indicative rather than
  converged.
- Only C-alpha atoms are scored; side-chain flexibility is not reported.
- Wall time is dominated by the minimisation of the implicit-solvent system
  (about 35 s per 100 steps on CPU); the operator is intended for short
  relaxation and flexibility screening, not production MD.
